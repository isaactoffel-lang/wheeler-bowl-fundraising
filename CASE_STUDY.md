## Work a handoff

Imagine an event organizer receives an offer of a workshop voucher. The outreach lead records the offer and passes collection to the person responsible for following up. The item stays out of the event inventory until someone has received it and checked its redemption terms.

| Stage | What has been established | Next action and responsible role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offered | A voucher was promised | Collection lead confirms delivery |
| Received | The voucher has arrived | Event lead checks redemption terms and confirms readiness |
| Ready | The event team can explain and present the item | Event lead adds it to event materials |

If the voucher arrives without clear redemption terms, it stays received and the event lead records the open question. That lets the team keep the contribution visible without presenting it as ready.

## Where the auction prototype fits

The prototype explored how items could be presented and how people could move through the interface. It did not implement shared bidding or payments. Inventory readiness belongs in the handoff process, where someone can confirm that the item exists and can be used.

The [blank tracker](handoff-template.csv) separates collection from readiness and gives each row a next action and owner. The public tracker and scenario illustrate the workflow. They contain no event records or fundraising outcomes. [Publication scope](PUBLICATION.md)
